# Genotype Scout

**Privacy-first analysis for the raw DNA file you already have.**

Genotype Scout is a third-party analysis tool. It does not test DNA. You bring a raw data file from a testing company (AncestryDNA, MyHeritage, 23andMe, FamilyTreeDNA), and Scout reads it entirely on your own device to show you what is in it.

**Use it live, nothing to install: [scout.writteninthegenome.blog](https://scout.writteninthegenome.blog)**

## Your file never leaves your device

Every calculation runs in your browser, on your machine. Your raw DNA file is never uploaded, never transmitted, and never stored on a server. Close the tab and nothing remains anywhere but with you. This is a design rule, not a setting.

## What Scout shows you

- **Ancestry composition** from a 25,000-marker dense reference panel, with Siberian, Oceanian, and Middle Eastern components resolved as their own signals
- **Ancestry Blend**: a two-stage continental and subpopulation read of your file
- **Chromosome painting** and PCA placement against reference populations
- **Maternal and paternal haplogroups** with a match quality score beside every call
- **Relationship tools built for endogamous families**, where shared segments can come from more than one ancestral couple
- **Health marker education**: what your markers are associated with, in plain language, with coverage honestly reported. Educational only, never medical advice
- **Native Roots**: a careful, evidence-weighted look at Indigenous American ancestry signals
- **Kit tools**: merge kits across companies with DNA SuperKit Builder, compare kits side by side

## Honest by construction

Scout reports how much of your file each result actually rests on. When a panel runs on too few of its markers, the result says so instead of printing a confident answer on thin data. A label must not outrun the measured data.
